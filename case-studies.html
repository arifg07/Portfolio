E-commerce & Growth

Navar Furniture — WooCommerce Build + Growth

Role: owned end-to-end · Jul 2025 — Present

The brief

Take ownership of a furniture brand's WordPress/WooCommerce storefront and grow its online visibility and sales, across both the build and the marketing.

The approach

Developed and optimized the WooCommerce storefront, then layered on-page, off-page and technical SEO on top — including individual product optimization — and designed high-converting landing pages. Google and Meta Ads drove traffic, while the catalog was extended onto Amazon UAE and Noon to capture marketplace demand. Email marketing (Mailchimp, Klaviyo), organic social and branded creatives and promo videos ran alongside the paid channels, with Google Analytics, Merchant Center and Business Profile used to monitor performance and visibility.

Stack & tools

WordPress, WooCommerce, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Google Merchant Center, Google Business Profile, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva.

This work was recognised with a Digital Marketing Excellence Award from Navar Furniture, May 2026.

Travel & Booking

Travlen — Travel Agency Website

Role: solo front-end build

The brief

Design a static travel agency site that showcases major destinations and gives visitors a simple way to start a booking enquiry, without the overhead of a full booking backend.

The approach

Built as a fast, static HTML/CSS/JS site so there was no server round-trip slowing down page loads. Prioritised large, high-quality destination imagery and a simple enquiry form, and tested the layout across desktop and mobile breakpoints so the experience held up on both.

Stack

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript — deployed as a static site.

Interior Design

Interior Design Showcase

Role: solo front-end build

The brief

Give an interior design brand a visual gallery site that helps visitors picture different design concepts for their own homes, and makes it easy to reach out for services.

The approach

Built the layout around large photography with minimal chrome, so the designs carry the page rather than UI decoration. Clear navigation was kept front and center, since the site's job is to move a visually engaged browser into an enquiry, not to entertain them indefinitely.

Stack

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript.

Marketplace / Full Stack

Labour Hiring Web Application

Role: front-end developer, built during Java Full Stack training

The brief

Build a platform that lets employers connect directly with local wage workers to get day-to-day tasks done, without going through an informal, word-of-mouth process.

The approach

Focused on a straightforward two-sided flow: employers post a task, workers can find and respond to it. Built the front-end to stay simple and legible for users who may not be comfortable with complex web apps, while the back-end (Java/Spring) handled the underlying data and matching logic.

Stack

React front-end, Java (Spring) back-end.
