E-commerce and growth

Navar Furniture: WooCommerce build and growth

Role: owned the build and the marketing · Jul 2025 - Present

The brief

Take over a furniture brand's WordPress and WooCommerce store, then grow its visibility and sales online. That meant doing the build and the marketing.

The approach

I developed and optimised the WooCommerce store first. On top of that I did on-page, off-page and technical SEO, including individual product pages, and designed landing pages meant to convert. Google and Meta ads brought in traffic. I also listed the catalog on Amazon UAE and Noon to reach people shopping on marketplaces. Email campaigns (Mailchimp and Klaviyo), social posts, branded creatives and promo videos ran alongside, and I tracked all of it in Google Analytics, Merchant Center and Business Profile.

Stack & tools

WordPress, WooCommerce, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Merchant Center, Business Profile, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva.

This work earned me a Digital Marketing Excellence Award from Navar Furniture in May 2026.

Travel and booking

Travlen: travel agency website

Role: solo front-end build

The brief

A static site for a travel agency that shows major destinations and lets visitors start a booking enquiry, without needing a full booking backend.

The approach

I built it as a plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript site so pages load fast, since there's no server in the way. Big destination photos come first, the enquiry form is simple, and I checked the layout on both desktop and mobile.

Stack

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, deployed as a static site.

Interior design

Interior design showcase

Role: solo front-end build

The brief

An interior design brand wanted a visual gallery site where visitors can picture different design ideas in their own homes, and easily get in touch.

The approach

I built the layout around large photos with very little interface around them, so the designs do the talking. Navigation stays clear and easy to find, because the goal is to turn someone who's browsing into an enquiry.

Stack

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript.

Marketplace · Full stack

Labour hiring web app

Role: front-end developer, built while learning Java full stack

The brief

A platform where employers can reach local daily-wage workers directly for everyday tasks, instead of relying on word of mouth.

The approach

The flow is simple on purpose: an employer posts a task and workers can find it and respond. I kept the front end plain and easy to read for people who aren't used to complicated web apps. The Java (Spring) back end handled the data and the matching.

Stack

React front end, Java (Spring) back end.
