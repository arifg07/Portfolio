SEO Jul 2026

Fix the product pages before you write blog posts

When someone asks me how to get a WooCommerce store ranking, I look at the product pages first. They're the pages that are supposed to rank and sell, so small fixes there pay off fastest.

My checklist is short. Does the title use words a buyer would actually type? Do the images have proper alt text? Does the page load quickly on a phone? Stores get slow fast once a few marketing plugins pile up on top of the theme.

Blog posts and backlinks matter too. I just do them after the basics are sorted.

Paid Ads Jun 2026

Google Ads and Meta Ads are not the same job

People search on Google when they already want something, so the ad only has to look like the right answer. On Meta nobody was looking for you. The ad has to stop the scroll before anything else can happen.

That changes what I make. Google ads lean on the wording. Meta ads lean on the creative, so the image or the video does most of the work.

It also changes how I judge results. Search traffic shows pretty quickly whether it converts. Social needs a longer look, because part of its value is people remembering the brand later.

Career May 2026

What it's like when the developer does the marketing too

I started out as a developer and moved into marketing later. These days I build the pages and I also read the numbers on them.

The nice part is how short the feedback loop gets. If a landing page isn't converting, I don't need a meeting to work out why. Sometimes the copy is wrong. Sometimes the page is just slow, and a marketer on their own might never spot that.

You don't have to master both. But if you're early in either one, spending a little time on the other side will make you better at your own job.
